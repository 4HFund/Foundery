The Mission

Affordable help with real dignity.

Rising costs shouldn’t steal anyone’s dignity. Kenny’s Kloset helps local families find useful essentials without pressure, judgment, or high prices.


Founded by Barbra and Jay Beatty, the ministry honors the legacy of the late Kenneth “Kenny” Hart by offering affordable clothing and practical kindness to every neighbor, no strings attached.

Donation Guidelines

Help us serve families well.

Good donations keep the ministry useful, organized, and dignified for every shopper.

We gladly accept

  • Clean clothing for all ages and sizes
  • Shoes in good usable condition
  • Coats, jackets, and seasonal items
  • Kids, maternity, and interview/formal clothing

Please avoid

  • Stained, torn, smoky, or heavily worn items
  • Broken shoes or unusable accessories
  • Household items unless specifically requested
  • Anything you would not feel good giving to a neighbor

Important note

  • Socks and underwear must be new only
  • Donation drop-off is 1st and 3rd Saturday
  • Drop-off time is 8 AM–12 PM
  • Message us for special donation questions

FAQ

Common Questions

Is everything really $1?

Yes. Clothes, shoes, bags, and similar items are $1 per item. Kenny’s Kloset is cash only.

Do I need to attend The Foundery?

No. Everyone is welcome. You do not need to be a church member to shop, donate, or ask questions.

When are you open?

Kenny’s Kloset is open the 1st and 3rd Saturday each month from 8 AM–12 PM.

Can you help with a specific need?

Message Kenny’s Kloset on Facebook for job interview clothes, school needs, formal events, or emergency situations.
