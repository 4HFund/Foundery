The Mission

Affordable help with real dignity.

Rising costs shouldn’t steal anyone’s dignity. After hearing a challenge to “use the gifts God gave you,” Barbra and Jay Beatty turned their retail know-how into a ministry.


Kenny’s Kloset honors the legacy of the late Kenneth “Kenny” Hart — beloved Brooke County teacher, principal, and coach — by offering affordable essentials to every neighbor, no strings attached.

All proceeds circle right back into helping local families, so every dollar you spend sparks another act of kindness.
Local. Simple. Welcoming.Serving Wellsburg, Brooke County, and the Ohio Valley.

Donation Guidelines

Help us serve families well.

Your donations keep Kenny’s Kloset stocked, affordable, and welcoming. These guidelines help our volunteers sort faster and keep the ministry useful.

We gladly accept

  • Clean clothing for all ages and sizes
  • Shoes in good usable condition
  • Coats, jackets, and seasonal items
  • Kids, maternity, and interview/formal clothing

Please avoid

  • Stained, torn, smoky, or heavily worn items
  • Broken shoes or unusable accessories
  • Household items unless specifically requested
  • Anything you would not feel good giving to a neighbor

Important note

  • Socks and underwear must be new only
  • Donation drop-off is 1st and 3rd Saturday
  • Drop-off time is 8 AM–12 PM
  • Message us for special donation questions

FAQ

Common Questions

Here are the answers most people need before visiting, donating, or sharing Kenny’s Kloset.

Is everything really $1?

Yes. Clothes and shoes are $1 per item. Kenny’s Kloset is cash only.

Do I need to attend The Foundery?

No. Everyone is welcome. You do not need to be a church member to shop, donate, or ask questions.

When are you open?

Kenny’s Kloset is open the 1st and 3rd Saturday each month from 8 AM–12 PM.

Can I donate clothing?

Yes. Clean, in-season clothing and shoes are welcome during donation drop-off hours.

Do you take socks and underwear?

Only if they are new. Used socks and underwear cannot be accepted.

Can you help with a specific need?

Message Kenny’s Kloset on Facebook for job interview clothes, school needs, formal events, or emergency situations.
